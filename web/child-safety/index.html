Child safety standards

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Findr is an adults-only (18+) social and dating service. We maintain zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including CSAM, grooming, and any use by minors.

Age requirement

Findr is not for anyone under 18. Users must attest they are 18+ at signup. We may remove accounts we believe belong to minors.

In-app reporting

Users can Report profiles and chat, including reason Suspected underage. Reports are stored for review. Child-safety reports trigger operator notification and review.

Enforcement

We remove violating content and accounts, preserve information as allowed by law, and report apparent CSAM to appropriate authorities (including NCMEC CyberTipline where required). Contact contactus@myfindr.fun (subject: Child safety).
