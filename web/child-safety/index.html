Child safety standards

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Findr is an adults-only (18+) social and dating service operated from the United States. We maintain zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, and any attempt by minors to use the service.

1. Age requirement

  • Findr is not directed to children and may not be used by anyone under 18.
  • Users must attest that they are 18 or older at signup.
  • We may suspend or remove accounts when we reasonably believe a user is under 18.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

Users must not post, request, or share:

  • CSAM or any content that sexualizes minors
  • Content that facilitates exploitation or grooming of minors
  • Profiles or messages indicating the user is under 18
  • Any other illegal content involving children

3. In-app reporting

Users can report child safety concerns inside the app using Report on profiles and in chat. Report reasons include Suspected underage, along with harassment, scams, non-consensual imagery, and other violations. Reports are sent to Findr for review and action.

4. Enforcement

When we become aware of CSAE or CSAM, we aim to:

  • Remove the content and disable the account involved
  • Preserve information as allowed by law for investigation
  • Report apparent CSAM to appropriate authorities, including the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via CyberTipline where we are required or believe it is necessary
  • Cooperate with law enforcement as required by applicable law

5. Moderation

Findr uses a combination of user reports, account restrictions, and human review (as resources allow during MVP). We may update detection and moderation practices over time.

6. Contact for child safety

For urgent child safety issues related to Findr, contact: contactus@myfindr.fun (subject line: Child safety).

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
