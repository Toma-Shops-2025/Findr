Delete your Findr account

Google Play and privacy laws require a clear way to delete your account and related data. Use the in-app control when signed in, or email us from the address on your account.

1. In the Findr app (preferred)

  1. Open Findr and sign in
  2. Go to the Safety tab
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account
  4. Confirm the dialog - this cannot be undone

Deletion soft-deletes your login, hides your profile from Nearby, and removes your stored coarse location. You will be signed out on the device.

2. By email

Email contactus@myfindr.fun from the email address on your Findr account. Subject line example: Delete Findr account.

Include the email you signed up with so we can locate the account. We will confirm when deletion is complete.

What we remove

  • Account login (email / password) so you cannot sign in again
  • Profile visibility in Nearby and public profile fields we scrub
  • Coarse location used for Nearby

Some records (for example safety reports or chat history visible to others) may be retained for a limited period for safety, abuse, or legal reasons. See the Privacy Policy.
