Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Findr is an adults-only (18+) social and dating app. These guidelines summarize what is allowed and what leads to removal. They work together with our Terms of Service and Child safety standards.

Be 18+

No minors. No content sexualizing minors. Report suspected underage users immediately using in-app Report.

Consent and respect

No harassment, hate, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual pressure. Findr welcomes straight and LGBTQ+ adults. Bigotry is not welcome.

Photos and media

Only post media you have the right to share. Consensual adult content may be allowed for adults 18+ in profiles and private chat, subject to these rules and applicable law. Never post anyone under 18, non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), threats, or illegal content.

No scams or spam

No phishing, fraud, fake profiles, or mass unsolicited promotion. Commercial use requires our permission.

Honesty

Do not impersonate others or misrepresent your age. Catfishing and fraud can result in a ban.

Reporting and enforcement

Use Block and Report in the app. We review reports and may warn, remove content, suspend, or ban accounts. Serious safety issues (underage users, threats, NCII) may be actioned without warning. Contact contactus@myfindr.fun for appeals or safety concerns.
